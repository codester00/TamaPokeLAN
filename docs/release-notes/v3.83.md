## v3.83 -- Meltan walks to Melmetal

**Meltan now evolves into Melmetal by walking**, a nod to Pokemon GO: 10,000
steps walked while Meltan is your live creature. Its card counts down the steps
left.

- Only steps walked while that Meltan is the live creature count. They stay with
  it: bank it, raise it again or trade it, and it keeps the steps it has walked.
- Its farewell does not wait for the walk.
- Melmetal no longer hatches from eggs: walk a Meltan there instead.

**Trading needs both boards on this version** (or later): a Meltan's steps travel
with it, and an older board would turn the trade down rather than lose them.

Nothing to do otherwise: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
