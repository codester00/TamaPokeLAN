Everything since v3.28, the last published release. Your creature, party, box,
bag, badges and Pokédex carry over.

## Before you update

- **Back up first.** "Backup, then install" on this page does it for you.
- **Leave "Erase device" unchecked.** It is the one thing that wipes a save.
- **Black screen after installing?** The board is usually waiting in its
  download mode, not broken. Hold PWR for about 10 seconds to switch it fully
  off, then press it again.
- **Do not go back below v3.31 afterwards.** Every creature gets its own ID on
  the first boot, and older versions cannot read the longer records: going back
  empties the party and the box. Keep your backup if you might.

## New in v3.34

**Training is a list of games now.** Tap the dumbbell and pick one; each row
says what it raises and how far along you already are. Some train one stat,
some split the same points between two (9 + 9 instead of 18 + 0), and three
raise joy and bond instead.

- Punching Bag (strength), Reaction Test (speed), Ball Game (defence)
- Rock Smash: tap as the ring lands on the rock (strength + speed)
- Block!: tap the side the attack comes from (defence + speed)
- Tilt Maze: tilt the board to roll the ball into the goal (speed + defence).
  If the ball rolls the wrong way on your board, please tell us.
- Boulder Push: tap while the needle is in the green (strength + defence)
- Berry Catch, Memory and Hide and Seek: play together for joy and bond

**Petting wears off.** Each tap on your creature gives less joy than the last
(10, 8, 6, 4, 2, then nothing) until you play one of the joy and bond games,
which makes it fresh again. The "So much fun!" message is gone.

**Hunger, energy, joy and hygiene fall much more slowly while awake.** Before,
they emptied in under an hour, so the only safe creature was a sleeping one.

- **The 8-hour rule.** Feed it, bathe it and play with it until all four needs
  are at 90 or more, and you can walk away for 8 hours. It will be hungry,
  tired and wanting attention when you come back, but it will not be punished
  and cannot run away. After 8 hours, neglect counts again.
- **One snack is not enough.** Only getting all four back above 90 starts a
  fresh 8 hours. Caring for it while it is already topped up keeps the 8 hours
  going.
- **Naps.** Put it to bed with the light during the day (6 am to 6 pm) and it
  gets up by itself once rested, about 15 minutes from tired. Fully cared for,
  it wakes with a fresh 8 hours, so you can put it down and go to school. If it
  was hungry or dirty, the nap does not change how much of the 8 hours is left.
  After 6 pm, bed is bedtime: it sleeps until you wake it.
- An existing creature starts with a fresh 8 hours the first time it boots on
  this version.

**The LAN BATTLE button is now LAN**, with HOST BATTLE, JOIN BATTLE and TRADE
inside, so trading is easier to find.

## New since v3.28

- **Trading.** Swap a party or box creature one for one with another board
  (LAN, then TRADE on both). If the connection drops mid-trade, nothing is lost
  or duplicated: both boards keep trying for 5 minutes, then each screen says
  what it holds so the two of you can decide.
- **Trade evolution.** Creatures that evolve by trade (Kadabra, Machoke,
  Graveler, Haunter and others) arrive marked and evolve the next time you
  raise them. Playing alone? The **Linking Cord** ($9,800 at the Mart) does the
  same for the creature you are raising.
- **Galar and Paldea gym ladders**, nine regions in all.
- **Fitness and real weight on the stat card**, in kilograms, from each
  species' real Pokédex weight.
- **Battles:** critical hits work as in the newer games (1.5x damage, and they
  ignore any stat changes that would weaken the hit), and Steel types cannot be
  poisoned.
- **LAN BATTLE taps that went back to the pet screen** are fixed: the button
  is larger, and a shaky tap on it no longer reads as a swipe.
- **Fixed a version of v3.33 that kept restarting** right after "thumbnails
  loaded". If your board did that, this update fixes it.
- **Restoring a backup** could be undone by the creature still on the screen
  when the board restarted. Fixed.
