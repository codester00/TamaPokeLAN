Fixes and tuning on top of v3.54. Your save carries over; back up first and
leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Wild creatures are less predictable.** Each one now knows four moves picked
  at random from everything it could know at its level, instead of always its
  four strongest. A high-level wild creature may know a TM move, but no longer
  always does. Watch what it uses, or catch it and look.
- **LAN battles count PP.** Both sides start with full PP and spend it as they
  fight, like a gym battle. Your own saved PP is not touched by a LAN fight.
  Both boards must be on v3.55 to battle each other.
- **Opponents that heal are back to their old selves.** The extra limit on
  healing from v3.52 is gone: PP already stops a creature healing forever.
- **The Game Corners sit where they belong in the story**, among the areas
  that open with the same badge, instead of at the top of the area list.
- **Every slot machine is different.** Each time you sit down at the slots the
  machine draws its own luck, from a little stingy to a little generous. If
  one is cold, get up and try again.
- **Both versions' prizes.** Celadon now also offers PINSIR, and Goldenrod
  SANDSHREW, beside the prizes already there.

Trading and LAN battles need both boards on this version.
