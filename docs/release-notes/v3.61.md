Bill's PC: your box is now Bill's PC, with room for six of every species. Your
save carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Bill's PC replaces the 18-slot box.** It keeps up to six of every species,
  over six thousand creatures in all, so you no longer have to release one to
  make room for the next catch. A seventh of the same species still needs a
  free party slot.
- **Your box moves in by itself** the first time the board starts on this
  version. If your box held more than six of one species, the extras stay
  where they were and are listed after the PC's pages, so nothing is lost.
- **For now the PC uses the box screen**, one page per species. Its own screens
  -- a grid of every species, and search by number -- come in the next update.
- **Backups include Bill's PC.** A backup with a full PC is much larger and can
  take about a minute to save or restore from the installer page. Backups made
  before this version still restore; your old box then moves into the PC again.
- **"Erase device" in the installer erases Bill's PC too**, just like your save.

Downgrading to an older version shows the box as it was on the day it moved.
Trading and LAN battles need both boards on v3.60 or later.
