## v3.71 -- the whole bag in battle

**The battle bag shows everything.** It used to show only the first four items
you could use in a fight, with no way to reach the rest -- carry four kinds of
Poke Ball and your potions were simply not there. Now it shows four items a
page, balls first, and the scrollbar on the right edge shows when there are
more: drag along the edge to turn the page, as on every other screen with pages.

**A Pokemon faints when the battle says so.** The losing Pokemon used to drop
out of view as soon as the moves were chosen, while "X used Y" was still on
screen and before its health bar had emptied. Now it stays standing through the
attack, its bar empties, and it drops out with the "fainted!" line, as in the
games. This applies to your Pokemon, the opponent's, and LAN battles.
