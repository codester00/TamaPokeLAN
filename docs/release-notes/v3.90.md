## v3.90 -- Sell at the Mart, and fewer items from wild battles

**You can sell now.** The top of the Poke Mart is a BUY | SELL switch. SELL
lists what your bag holds and how many of each, and the Mart pays half its
price -- a spare stone found in the wild is $1,050, a Linking Cord $4,900. It
asks before it sells. The Master Ball, which the Mart never sells, it never
buys either. A sale that would push your wallet past $999,999 is refused
instead of losing the money.

**Wild battles drop fewer items.** A win now gives one item, and sometimes a
second: one time in four on easy, one in two on hard. It used to be one or two
on easy and two or three on hard, which filled the bag within days. Stones and
TMs still turn up as before, on top.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save and your bag carry over unchanged.
