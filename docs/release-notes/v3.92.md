## v3.92 -- PP Up

**New at the Poke Mart: PP Up, $10,000.** Use one from the bag, pick the
creature (from your party, or the one you are raising) and then one of its
moves: that move gets a fifth more PP, up to three times. TACKLE goes from 35 to
42, 49 and finally 56. The extra PP stays with the creature wherever it goes,
including trades and LAN battles. If you teach that slot a new move later, the
new move keeps the PP Ups.

**Both boards need this version to trade or battle each other.** An older
board will refuse the link rather than get the numbers wrong -- update both.

Nothing else to do: your save carries over unchanged.
