## TamaPoke v4.10

**The tournament is a real endgame tournament now.**

- **Every opponent brings a full team of six at level 100.** They keep the Pokemon you know them for, fully evolved (Brock's Geodude is a Golem now), and fill the rest of their team with Pokemon of their favourite type. Each trainer always brings the same team.
- **They bring as many Pokemon as you do**, so a team of three faces three.
- **Story trainers fight harder the later they came in their story.** Early gym leaders are softer, champions are tough. A well-raised team should win the title a little over half the time.

**The Battle Hall is rebalanced so a clear is something to be proud of.**

- **Opponents never outlevel your Pokemon.** They start three levels below and only catch up at the very last battle.
- **Opponents are never perfect.** A Pokemon with great IVs has a real edge, and type matchups matter most.
- A well-trained Pokemon with average IVs should clear all 18 about one run in ten.

Nothing to do: your save, records and marks are untouched. Trading and LAN battles work with v4.08 and v4.09 boards.
