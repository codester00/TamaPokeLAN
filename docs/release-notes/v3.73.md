## v3.73 -- the real slot machines

**The Game Corner slots now play like the originals.** Celadon's machines are
Red's and Goldenrod's are Crystal's, rules and all. Before, both were tuned to
hand back a little under or over a coin per coin, which made wins rare and
small -- thirty spins in a row with nothing was common at Celadon.

- **Celadon:** wins come about one spin in ten at 1 coin, and one in three at 3.
  Every so often the machine gets into a mood for 7s and BARs and stays that
  way until one pays -- and three 7s can keep it going. One machine is lucky
  each time you walk in; you will not know which, but it shows.
- **Goldenrod:** stingy on one line, generous at 2 or 3 coins. When the second
  reel races to line up 7s, or the last reel crawls, or Golem and Chansey turn
  up, the machine has already decided. Lucky machines are much more common
  here than at Celadon.
- Like the originals, both machines pay out more than they take over a long
  session, mostly in streaks. The prize counters keep the games' prices.

Nothing to do: update, and sit down at a machine.
