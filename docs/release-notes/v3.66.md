## v3.66 -- Bill's PC starts on every board

**Bill's PC could fail to start on a board that had other software on it
before.** You would see a small red warning triangle next to the battery, every
region in the PC read 0, and when a Pokemon said farewell with your party full,
the only choices were to release it or release one of your party. This update
sets the PC up on its first start, and the triangle goes away.

**Your old box is safe either way.** If the PC ever can't start, your old box
takes its place again: farewells and catches go there when the party is full,
and the button in the middle of the party screen opens it. Once the PC starts,
the box moves into it as before.

The PC's region list is now titled BILL'S PC instead of showing your Pokedex
count.

Nothing to do: update, and the PC sets itself up at the next start.
