Galar is a real place to explore, and its legendaries have homes. Your save
carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Galar's 39 areas**, taken from Sword and Shield -- the Slumbering Weald,
  Galar's routes and mines, Glimwood Tangle, Steamdrift Way, Circhester Bay
  and every zone of the Wild Area, from Rolling Fields to the Lake of Outrage.
  Each opens with the gym badge that fits it, with levels that follow Galar's
  gym leaders. A locked area says which badge it needs, like
  REQUIRES FIRE BADGE.
- **The Wild Area's weather is all there.** Each zone holds the creatures of
  every kind of weather it has in the game.
- **Strong wanderers roam the Wild Area.** Now and then a zone turns up one of
  the powerful creatures that wander it in the game, well above the zone's
  usual level.
- **Galar's legendaries live somewhere now.** Zacian, Zamazenta and Eternatus
  wait for you after you become champion. The first visit to a legendary's
  home after it opens is a guaranteed meeting, and it keeps turning up now and
  then until you catch it.
- **Storyline progression locks adapted from the real games' data** reach
  Galar too.

Paldea keeps the old whole-region encounters for now.
