## v3.91 -- Stronger vitamins, and X items for battle

**Vitamins give +10 now, and you can give them to your party.** Protein, Iron
and Carbos used to add only +5, and only to the creature you were raising --
less than a quick training game. Now each gives +10, and when you have a party
the bag asks who should get it: tap a party member, or the middle of the ring
for the creature you are raising. Building a team for hard mode no longer means
swapping each one in to train it. Each stat still stops at the limit that
creature's own strength allows.

**New at the Poke Mart: X Attack, X Defense, X Sp. Atk, X Sp. Def and X
Speed.** Use one from the bag during a battle and that stat rises sharply (two
stages) for as long as your creature stays in, up to the usual maximum. Like any
item it takes your turn. They cost $1,000, or $2,000 for the two defenses, and
they can push a creature past anything training can reach -- a way to take on a
tough leader with a well-stocked bag.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
