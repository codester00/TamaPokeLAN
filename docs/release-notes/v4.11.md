## TamaPoke v4.11

**The Battle Hall now puts your toughest matchups first.**

- **Your Pokemon's counters come early, the types it beats come late.** A Charizard meets Rock right at the start and Grass near the end. Losing to an obvious counter now happens while the stakes are low and the opponents are weakest, instead of ending a long run near the top.
- **The late opponents are still the strongest**, so even a good matchup is a real fight at the end.
- **Opponents are a little stronger overall** to balance this out: a well-trained Pokemon with average IVs still clears all 18 about one run in ten.
- The order is set when the run starts, so evolving partway through does not change it.

**The PLAZA's LAN TRADE button now matches the other buttons** instead of standing out in yellow.

Nothing to do: your save, records and marks are untouched. A Hall run already in progress finishes in the order it started with; the next one uses the new order. Trading and LAN battles work with v4.08 to v4.10 boards.
