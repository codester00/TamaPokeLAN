  creatures and gym leaders always start full. LAN battles do not use PP.
- **Gym leaders no longer heal turn after turn.** A leader with Recover used to
  heal every time it was a little hurt, which could drag a fight out for 25
  turns or more. It now heals only when the heal actually gains on you, never
  more than three times in a row, and less often the more it has healed.
