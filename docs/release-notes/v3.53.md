The Game Corners. Your save carries over; back up first and leave "Erase
device" unchecked, as always.

- **Celadon Game Corner** is at the top of Kanto's Explore list once you have
  beaten Lt. Surge, and **Goldenrod Game Corner** at the top of Johto's once you
  have beaten Bugsy. Tap one to go in.
- **Buy coins with your step money**: 50 coins for $1,000.
- **Slots** with the original reels and payouts — Red's machines at Celadon,
  Crystal's at Goldenrod. Bet 1, 2 or 3 coins to play more lines.
- **Card flip** at Goldenrod, with Crystal's odds.
- **Prize counters**: trade coins for Abra, Clefairy, Dratini, Scyther and
  Porygon at Celadon, or Abra, Ekans and Dratini at Goldenrod, and for TMs —
  including Surf at Celadon. A prize creature joins your party or box.

Trading and LAN battles need both boards on this version.
