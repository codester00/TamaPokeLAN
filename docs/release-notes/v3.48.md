Galar's Wild Area has weather now, the way it did in Sword and Shield. Your
save carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Each Wild Area zone has its own weather, and it changes every day** --
  clear, overcast, rain, thunderstorms, snow, snowstorms, harsh sun,
  sandstorms or fog, picked from the zone's own weathers in the game and kept
  until midnight. The Explore screen shows each zone's weather for today
  beside its levels, so you know who might be out before you go.
- **The weather changes who you meet.** Some creatures only come out in their
  weather: Mimikyu and Sylveon in the fog, Riolu and Weavile in a snowstorm,
  Umbreon in a sandstorm, Jolteon in a thunderstorm, Vaporeon in the rain, and
  plenty more. If one you want is not around, check again tomorrow -- every
  weather comes round.
- **It needs the clock set.** If the board's clock was never set, every zone
  mixes all its weathers together instead. You can set the clock from the
  settings screen.
