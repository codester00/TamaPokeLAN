## TamaPoke v4.05

**Gauntlet challengers switch.** From the eleventh challenger of a gauntlet run, an opponent whose creature is losing the type matchup may pull it out for a teammate that does better, as trainers do in the games. They play fair: a creature always stays in for its first few turns, they never switch into something your creature hits hard, and switching takes their turn, so they don't attack that turn. Earlier challengers and everyone outside the Battle Zone never switch.

Nothing to do: your save is untouched.
