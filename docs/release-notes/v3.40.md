The legendaries have homes, and Johto is a real place to explore. Your save
carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Legendaries live somewhere now.** Articuno waits in Seafoam Islands, Zapdos
  in the Power Plant, Moltres on Mt. Ember and Mewtwo in Cerulean Cave; in
  Johto, Raikou, Entei and Suicune start at the Burned Tower, Lugia is in the
  Whirl Islands and Ho-Oh in Tin Tower. Each appears once you have the badge
  the games asked for, at the level the games fought it at.
- **Your first visit is guaranteed.** The first time you explore a
  legendary's home after earning its badge, you meet it. Win, lose or run --
  that one is spent, but it keeps turning up now and then in the same place
  until you catch it. Raikou and Entei roam: once met, they can turn up
  anywhere in Johto. A home with a legendary still waiting has a star on its
  row.
- **Legendaries are caught, not hatched.** Eggs no longer hold the legendaries
  that have homes. Mythical creatures like Mew and Celebi, which have no home
  in any game's story, still come only from eggs, as rarely as before.
- **Johto's 35 areas**, taken from Crystal -- Route 29, Sprout Tower, Ilex
  Forest, the National Park, the Burned Tower, the Ice Path, the Whirl Islands,
  Mt. Silver and the rest -- each opening with the badge that opened it in the
  game, with levels that follow Johto's gym leaders.
- **Day and night matter in Johto.** In Crystal's grass some creatures only
  come out at night, like Hoothoot, and some only in the morning, like Ledyba;
  Johto's areas follow the board's clock. If the clock was never set, it
  counts as day.
- **Headbutt trees** are in: Heracross, Aipom and Pineco, which lived only in
  trees in Crystal, can be found where those trees were.

A few of Johto's creatures were never wild in Crystal's Johto -- Mareep,
Houndour and Murkrow among them -- so they still come from eggs. Other regions keep the old
whole-region encounters for now.
