## v3.78 -- swarms

Some days, one place in a region is overrun by a single kind of creature, the
way the games' swarms and outbreaks worked. Each region has one swarm a day, and
it changes at midnight.

- **Johto**: Dunsparce in Dark Cave, Yanma on Route 35, and -- once you can fish
  for the best catches -- Qwilfish on Route 32.
- **Hoenn**: Emerald's TV outbreaks -- Seedot on Routes 102, 117 and 120, Nuzleaf
  on Route 114 and Skitty on Route 116. On its day the outbreak creature is
  half of what you meet in the route's grass, as in Emerald.
- **Sinnoh**: Platinum's 22 swarms, from Doduo on Route 201 to Larvitar on
  Route 206 and Beldum on Route 228. They start once you have beaten the
  champion, when Platinum hands you the National Pokedex.

An area with today's swarm says **SWARM** on its row in Explore, after the
level band (and after the weather, where there is one). The board needs its
clock set for swarms: with no clock there are none.

Crystal's Remoraid swarm on Route 44 is not here, because it never happened in
the game either: Route 44 fishes from a different pond than the one the swarm
swaps.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
