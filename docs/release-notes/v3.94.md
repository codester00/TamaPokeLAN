## v3.94 -- the Pokemon League is one run

**The Elite Four and the champion are now fought in one go, as in the games.**
Challenging the first Elite Four member starts a League run. Win, and the
League screen offers NEXT (the next member, same team), BAG and GIVE UP.

**Your HP comes back for every fight, but your PP does not.** During a run you
cannot put your creature to bed, and PP does not refill. Plan your moves, and
bring PP Ups.

**Lose, give up, or take longer than an hour, and the run is over.** The badges
you won are yours to keep, but the next run starts again at the first Elite
Four member (who pays half, since you already hold that badge). Beat the
champion and the run ends in triumph.

**New: ETHER.** A rare find after wild battles: it gives one move 10 PP back.
Use it from the bag between League fights, or any time.

Nothing to do: your save and your badges carry over unchanged.
