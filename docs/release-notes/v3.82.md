## v3.82 -- evolution branches

Several creatures now choose between evolutions, as in the games:

- **Tyrogue** (level 20) becomes Hitmonlee if its Attack is higher than its
  Defense, Hitmonchan if it is lower, and Hitmontop if they are equal. Train it
  the way you want it to go.
- **Wurmple** (level 7) becomes Silcoon or Cascoon, and **Cosmoem** (level 53)
  Solgaleo or Lunala -- an even chance, fixed for each creature from the day it
  hatches. If you already own Solgaleo, your Cosmoem always becomes Lunala, and
  the other way round. A Solgaleo or Lunala you get by evolving counts as
  caught: it stops appearing in Explore, and the other one's meeting is still
  waiting for you there.
- **Applin** and **Charcadet** let you pick at level 30: tap EVOLVE and choose
  Flapple, Appletun or Dipplin, or Armarouge or Ceruledge. KEEP waits for the
  next level, as always.
- When **Nincada** becomes Ninjask and you have a Poke Ball in your bag and room
  for one more creature, a **Shedinja** appears too and joins your party (or
  Bill's PC). The ball is used up.

The evolution question now says what your creature will become ("Evolve into
Raichu?").

Hitmonchan, Hitmontop, Cascoon, Shedinja, Appletun, Dipplin and Ceruledge no
longer hatch from eggs.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
