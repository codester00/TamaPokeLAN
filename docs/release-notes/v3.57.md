Battle fixes, most of them found in the first LAN battles. Your save carries
over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

**Both boards need this version for a LAN battle.** An older board is refused
rather than allowed into a fight it would get out of step with. Trading still
works with v3.55 and later.

- **LAN battles keep both boards in step after a knockout.** When a creature
  fainted, the board running the battle picked its replacement but never told
  the other board, so the second player could be choosing a move for a creature
  that was no longer out. Both boards now bring on the same creature, with the
  same health.
- **Poison and burn damage reach the other board in LAN battles.** Damage taken
  at the end of a turn used to show up a turn late on the second board, and a
  creature knocked out by it never fainted there at all.
- **The POKEMON menu shows your whole team.** With six in a fight it only
  showed four slots, one of them the creature already out, so your last two
  could never be switched in. The five you can switch to are all there now,
  the fifth just above the others.
- **A creature with no attacks can STRUGGLE.** An Abra, which knows no attack,
  had nothing to press and the fight could not go on. Now it struggles, as in
  the games -- in every kind of battle, not just LAN.
- **The battle scene stays put.** It used to change every time a new creature
  was sent out. It is now set when the fight starts, and in a LAN battle both
  players see the same place.
- **A knocked-out creature is replaced by the next one still standing.** After
  switching around, a faint could end the fight while teammates were still
  able to battle, or send in one that had already fainted.
- **Winning a gym battle no longer cuts away early.** Picking the move that won
  the fight jumped straight to the badge screen, so the attack, the health bar
  draining and the last creature fainting were never seen. Now the final turn
  plays out like every other one, and the badge screen opens when you tap past
  the last message.

The bag stays unavailable in LAN battles, as in the games' link battles.
