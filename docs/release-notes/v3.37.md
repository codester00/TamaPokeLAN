Small fixes to the training games on top of v3.36. Your save carries over;
back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Boulder Push is slower.** The needle swings about 25% slower, so the green
  can be hit on the board's touch screen. A full session is now 45 steps
  instead of 60, so a good round still trains as much as before.
- **"+0 joy +0 bond" now says why.** Memory, Hide and Seek and Berry Catch were
  paying all along, but joy stops at 100 and bond can only rise 20 a day. A
  creature that was already happy, and had already had its bond for the day,
  got nothing, and the screen just said +0. It now says **JOY MAX** or **BOND
  MAX TODAY**. The training games say **MAX** the same way for a stat that is
  already as high as it goes.
- **Berry Catch rocks look like rocks.** The grey balls were rocks, and
  catching one costs a point. They are now dark and lumpy with a red cross, a
  caught one flashes −1 over the basket, and the opening hint reads "CATCH
  BERRIES, DODGE ROCKS!".

Everything new in v3.34 (the training games, the slower needs and the 8-hour
rule, naps, the LAN menu) is in the v3.34 notes.
