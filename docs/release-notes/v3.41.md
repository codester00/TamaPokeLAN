Fishing now follows the games' own progression. Your save carries over; back
up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **The best fishing catches wait for the Super Rod.** In the games you only
  fish up creatures like Gyarados, Horsea or Krabby once you have been given
  the best rod. Explore now does the same: in Kanto they start turning up after
  the Rainbow Badge, and in Johto after you become champion. Until then each
  area's other creatures fill in, at their usual rates.
- There is nothing on the screen to tell you -- the areas look exactly as
  before. Keep exploring after your badges and they will appear.

Other regions keep the old whole-region encounters for now.
