## TamaPoke v3.99

**Shiny eggs are rare again.** A well-cared-for creature's farewell egg was shiny about one time in ten, so shinies were turning up every few weeks. Now a farewell egg is shiny about one time in a hundred, and the best care (a long daily streak and a strong bond) makes that four times likelier: about one shiny every few months for a player who looks after every creature to its farewell. Eggs from other endings are half as likely again. Shinies you already have are untouched.
