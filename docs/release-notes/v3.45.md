Kalos is a real place to explore, and its legendaries have homes. Your save
carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Kalos's 30 areas**, taken from X and Y -- Santalune Forest, the road to
  Camphrier and Cyllage, Reflection Cave, the Lumiose Badlands, the Lost
  Hotel, Frost Cavern, Terminus Cave, Victory Road and the rest -- each opening
  with the badge that opened it in the game, with levels that follow Kalos's
  gym leaders. A locked area says which badge it needs, like
  REQUIRES CLIFF BADGE.
- **Flower beds, rough terrain and hordes count.** The creatures you met in
  Kalos's coloured flower beds and rough ground are in the grass, and the ones
  that came in hordes of five now turn up now and then on their own.
- **Kalos's legendaries live somewhere now.** Xerneas and Yveltal wait after
  the Psychic Badge, Zygarde after you become champion. The first visit to a
  legendary's home after its badge is a guaranteed meeting, and it keeps
  turning up now and then until you catch it.
- **Kalos's legendaries are caught, not hatched.** Diancie, Hoopa and
  Volcanion still come only from eggs.
- **An area with nothing to show says so.** If none of an open area's
  creatures can be drawn yet, it stays grey and reads NO VALID SPRITES instead
  of letting you in to find nothing. It will light up by itself once its
  creatures can be shown.
- **Storyline progression locks adapted from the real games' data** reach
  Kalos too.

Alola and the regions after it keep the old whole-region encounters for now.
