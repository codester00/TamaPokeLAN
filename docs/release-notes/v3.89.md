## v3.89 -- The Pokedex shows what you have

**A species is drawn in colour once it has been yours**: one you raised, one
you have right now in your party or Bill's PC, or a legendary you caught --
even if you later traded it away. Everything else is a silhouette. Before,
only creatures you had raised were in colour, so your catches looked missing.

**The green dot now means "registered"**: you raised that creature, and its
Pokedex entry is complete. Before, the dot meant "you have one right now".
A creature's own page shows the same dot beside its name.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
