## v3.69 -- Smeargle learns Sketch

**Smeargle could not learn a single move.** In the real games its only move is
Sketch, and this game did not have it, so a Smeargle reached level 35 knowing
nothing and could only Struggle in a fight.

**Sketch is here now.** Smeargle gets one Sketch at level 1 and another at 11,
21 and 31, up to four. In a battle, using Sketch copies the move your opponent
used last, with full PP, and Smeargle keeps that move for good, so you build its
moveset by choosing what to fight. Sketch fails if the opponent has not moved
yet, or if Smeargle already knows that move. It works in LAN battles too.

**Wild Smeargle now know four random moves**, and keep them if you catch one.

**If you already have a Smeargle:** update, and it fills its empty move slots
with Sketch by itself (four at level 31 and up). You can also pick Sketch from
its move list.

Both boards need this update to battle each other over LAN.
