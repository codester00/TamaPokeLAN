Kanto is a real place to explore now. Your save carries over; back up first
and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Kanto's real routes, caves and islands.** Exploring Kanto used to be one
  big lucky dip: any Kanto creature, anywhere, all equally likely. Now it is
  split into 36 areas taken from FireRed and LeafGreen -- Route 1, Viridian
  Forest, Mt. Moon, Rock Tunnel, the Safari Zone, the Sevii Islands, Cerulean
  Cave and the rest -- each with the creatures the games put there, as common
  or as rare as they were there. Both versions are included, so nothing is
  locked to one version.
- **Areas open as you earn badges.** Tap the area row on the Explore screen
  to see the list. Route 1 and Viridian Forest are open from the start; Mt.
  Moon waits for the Boulder Badge, the Safari Zone for the Rainbow Badge, and
  Cerulean Cave for the champion. A locked area tells you what it needs, for
  example "REQUIRES BOULDER BADGE". Badges from either difficulty count.
- **Wild levels follow the gyms, not your pet.** An area's creatures sit just
  below the next gym leader's level, so Route 1 is levels 1-12 and Victory
  Road 44-52, however strong your own creature has grown. A few creatures
  appear above their area's levels where the games had them that way, like
  the Gyarados you can fish up on Route 22.
- **Hard now means a smarter opponent, not a stronger one.** On hard, a wild
  creature fights with the smart battle AI at the same levels as on easy, and
  still drops two items when you win. It used to be any level from 1 to 100.

Other regions still use the old whole-region encounters for now; they get
their own areas next. Kanto's legendary birds and Mewtwo are not in the
areas yet either -- they are coming back with fixed homes of their own.
