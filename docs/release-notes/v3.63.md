Bill's PC gets its own screens, and the Pokedex learns what you own. Your save
carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Bill's PC has its own screens.** The middle of the party ring opens it: pick a
  region, and every species shows on a grid -- in colour with how many you keep
  there, outlined if only your party or the creature you are raising has one,
  grey if you have none. Tap a species to see its six and move them to your party
  or release them.
- **Depositing is one tap.** Tap a creature in your party, then the middle of the
  ring, and it goes into the PC with the rest of its species.
- **A seventh of a species no longer has to be let go.** With a full party, the
  PC button opens that species' six so you can choose which one makes room.
- **A trade that could never finish is stopped before it starts.** If the creature
  you would receive has nowhere to go, the trade screen says NO ROOM and ACCEPT
  does nothing, instead of the trade getting stuck.
- **GO TO #.** Tap the footer of the Pokedex or the PC and type a number to jump
  straight to that species.
- **OWNED in the Pokedex.** A green dot marks every species you own right now, and
  the region list counts them.
- **MISSING ONLY** in the PC lists just the species you do not own yet.

Trading and LAN battles need both boards on v3.60 or later.
