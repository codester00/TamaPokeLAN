New looks for you, and a proper ending when a fight goes wrong. Your save
carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **19 trainer avatars.** Every trainer class from the original games is now
  playable: Beauty, Biker, Channeler, Fisher, Gambler, Gentleman, Rocker,
  Rocket, Scientist, Super Nerd and Swimmer join the eight you already had.
  Tap your avatar on the trainer card to open the new picker, drag along the
  rim for the next page, and tap the one you want. Whoever you picked before
  is still who you are.
- **Blacking out.** Losing a gym or wild battle used to end on one more line of
  text. Now the screen goes dark, you black out, and Mom is waiting at home to
  tell you your team is all better. Tap to wake up next to your creature.
  Nothing is taken from you, and LAN battles with a friend never black out.

If you ever go back to an older version, anyone wearing one of the new avatars
becomes RED again.
