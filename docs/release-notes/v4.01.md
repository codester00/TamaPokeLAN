## TamaPoke v4.01

**The screens are in a new order.** Swiping left from your creature now goes to your party, then the Poke Mart, the Pokedex and your player card. Swiping right goes to Explore, the gyms, and a new last stop, the **PLAZA**. Your own things are on the left, battles are on the right, and the screens you use most are closest to home.

**LAN battles and trading have moved to the PLAZA.** They used to sit behind a LAN button on the gym screen, and trading was a button inside that. Now the PLAZA has a **LAN BATTLE** button and a **LAN TRADE** button side by side, and the gym screen only lists the gyms. The PLAZA also shows two buttons that don't work yet: the **Battle Hall** and the **Battle Zone**, which are coming in a later update. The Battle Zone tells you what it will need: every region's champion beaten.

Nothing to do: your save, your badges and your creatures are untouched.
