## TamaPoke v4.03

**The Battle Zone opens.** Beat every region's champion, on easy or hard, and the Battle Zone button on the PLAZA lights up. This version is its first part. It shows your Battle Points (BP), the zone's own currency, and its prize counter, where BP buy items: a Master Ball, PP Ups, Ethers, vitamins, the Linking Cord and every evolution stone. The tournament and the gauntlet, where you will earn BP, are marked COMING SOON and arrive in later updates.

Nothing to do: your save is untouched, and your BP start at zero.
