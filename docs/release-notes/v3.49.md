Paldea is a real place to explore -- the last region -- and its legendaries
have homes. Your save carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device"
unchecked, as always.

- **Paldea's 32 zones**, from Scarlet and Violet: the Province areas from
  South to North, Asado Desert, Tagtree Thicket, Glaseado Mountain, Casseroya
  Lake, the caves, the four seas and Area Zero. Paldea is an open world, so
  each zone opens with the badge that fits how strong its creatures are,
  climbing from the south to the north as the game does. A locked zone says
  which badge it needs, like REQUIRES WATER BADGE.
- **Real odds.** Each zone's creatures turn up as often as they do in the
  game, from the game's own spawn table: Lechonk and Fletchling are everywhere
  in the south, Sandile and Cacnea rule the desert.
- **Weather and time of day.** Paldea's zones have daily weather like Galar's
  Wild Area, shown on the Explore screen, and its night creatures come out at
  night.
- **Paldea's legendaries live somewhere now.** The four Treasures of Ruin wait
  at their shrines, and Koraidon and Miraidon in Area Zero, after you become
  champion. The first visit to a legendary's home after it opens is a
  guaranteed meeting, and it keeps turning up now and then until you catch it.
- **The expansions stay in eggs.** Kitakami's and Blueberry Academy's
  creatures and legendaries, and Pecharunt, still come only from eggs.
- **Storyline progression locks adapted from the real games' data** reach
  Paldea too.

Every region now has real places to explore.
