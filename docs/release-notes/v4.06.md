## TamaPoke v4.06

**The tournament opens in the Battle Zone.** Sixteen trainers, four rounds, single elimination: you against gym leaders, Elite Four members and champions drawn from every region you have the sprites for.

- **Bring any team of one to six.** Each match caps your creatures' levels at your opponent's highest, the same rule as the hard gyms, so a level 100 team still has a real fight.
- **The bracket is seeded**: you start against the weakest and only meet the strongest in the final. The other matches are fought out in the background, so the same pairing can go either way.
- **Opponents use their full strength**, and in the semi-final and final they switch out of a bad matchup.
- **PP carries through the whole tournament**, for you and your opponents. HP is restored before every match.
- **Your tournament is saved between rounds.** Turning the board off in the middle of a match counts as losing it. Every win pays Battle Points, and winning the final adds to your tournament record.

Nothing to do: your save is untouched.
