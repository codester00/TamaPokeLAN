## v3.85 -- Stones in every region

**Evolution stones can now be found in all nine regions**, not just the first
four: Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar and Paldea each have their own places where
stones turn up after a win, taken from where the original games leave them
lying around. The Ice Stone is now found too (Alola, Galar and Paldea).

- **Stones are rarer**: about one win in 25 in a stone's area finds one (it was
  about one in ten in v3.84), on easy and hard alike. Everywhere else a win
  never finds a stone.
- **The Safari Zone and the Great Marsh** now hand out their Leaf Stones: a
  successful catch there can bring one, since a catch is how a Safari encounter
  is won.
- The Mart still sells every stone. The Black Augurite is only sold there.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
