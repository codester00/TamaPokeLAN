Battles where training matters the way it should. Your save carries over; back
up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

**Both boards need this version for a LAN battle**, because the two boards now
work out battle stats the same new way. Trading still works with v3.55 and later.

- **Training counts in proportion to level in battle.** A fully trained
  creature used to get the whole +100 at any level, so a young creature with
  maxed training flattened gyms meant for its level -- including on hard. Now
  training is worth its full +100 at level 100, +50 at level 50 and +14 at
  level 14, just like the individual values already were.
- **The training screen and the minigames still show training from 0 to 100.**
  Nothing you trained is lost. The numbers on the stat card are the ones your
  creature fights with, so they are lower than before for young creatures.
- **Hard gym leaders are fully trained.** Easy leaders fight as the games'
  story leaders do, with no training; hard leaders bring fully trained teams.
  Hard mode is meant to be hard again: bring a team that covers the leader's
  type.
