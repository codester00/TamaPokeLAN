## v3.88 -- A clearer Pokedex, and timers that never stick

**The Pokedex has a BACK button.** Every Pokedex screen -- the region list,
the grid, a creature's page and the number pad -- now shows BACK in the same
place at the bottom, and it always goes up one level. Before, tapping the top
of the grid closed the whole Pokedex and dropped you on your pet, and the
bottom of the grid did something different from the bottom of every other
screen.

**GO TO # moved to the top of the grid**, under the region name, with the
page number beside it.

**Fixed: odd symbols at the bottom of the Pokedex.** The page number there
printed random characters instead of "3/10". It reads properly now.

**Fixed: timers on a board left running for weeks.** After about seven weeks
without a restart, a farewell could hang on screen, and a minigame or a
dialog could end at once or not at all. Every timer now keeps working
however long the board stays on.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
