Hoenn is a real place to explore, and its legendaries have homes. Your save
carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Hoenn's 43 areas**, taken from Emerald -- Route 101, Petalburg Woods,
  Granite Cave, the Route 111 desert, Mt. Pyre, the Safari Zone, the open sea,
  the seabed, Sky Pillar, Victory Road and the rest -- each opening with the
  badge that opened it in the game, with levels that follow Hoenn's gym
  leaders. A locked area says which badge it needs, like REQUIRES HEAT BADGE.
- **Hoenn's legendaries live somewhere now.** Regirock, Regice and Registeel
  appear once Dive would open the Sealed Chamber; Kyogre, Groudon and
  Rayquaza, and the roaming Latias and Latios, after you become champion. As in
  Kanto and Johto, the first visit to a legendary's home after its badge is a
  guaranteed meeting, and it keeps turning up now and then until you catch it.
- **Hoenn's legendaries are caught, not hatched.** Eggs no longer hold them.
  Jirachi and Deoxys, which have no home in the story, still come only from
  eggs.
- **Storyline progression locks adapted from the real games' data** reach
  Hoenn too.
- Kanto's Altering Cave now holds what it holds in the game, Zubat, rather
  than a set the game only unlocks through a special event.

Other regions keep the old whole-region encounters for now.
