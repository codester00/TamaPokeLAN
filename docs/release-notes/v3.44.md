Unova is a real place to explore, and its legendaries have homes. Your save
carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Unova's 43 areas**, taken from Black 2 and White 2 -- Route 19, Floccesy
  Ranch, Castelia, the Desert Resort, Chargestone Cave, Reversal Mountain, the
  Giant Chasm, Victory Road, and after you become champion Black and White's
  old Unova: the Dreamyard, Pinwheel Forest, Dragonspiral Tower and more. Each
  opens with the badge that opened it in the game, with levels that follow
  Unova's gym leaders. A locked area says which badge it needs, like
  REQUIRES BOLT BADGE.
- **Shaking grass and dust clouds count.** Audino, Drilbur, Emolga and the
  other creatures that only appear in Unova's rustling grass, dust clouds,
  bridge shadows and rippling water now turn up now and then while you
  explore.
- **Unova's legendaries live somewhere now.** Cobalion, Virizion and Terrakion
  wait on Routes 13, 11 and 22; Reshiram, Zekrom and Kyurem after you become
  champion. The first visit to a legendary's home after its badge is a
  guaranteed meeting, and it keeps turning up now and then until you catch it.
- **Unova's legendaries are caught, not hatched.** Tornadus, Thundurus and
  Landorus, which Black 2 and White 2 never let you meet, still come only from
  eggs, like Victini, Keldeo, Meloetta and Genesect.
- **Storyline progression locks adapted from the real games' data** reach
  Unova too.

Kalos and the regions after it keep the old whole-region encounters for now.
