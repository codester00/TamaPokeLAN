## v3.81 -- evolution stones

**The ten evolution stones are in the Poke Mart**, at $2,100 each: Fire, Water,
Thunder, Leaf, Moon, Sun, Shiny, Dusk, Dawn and Ice. Use one from the bag on
your live creature and it evolves on the spot, at any level, as in the games.

- **A stone evolution now needs its stone.** Pikachu, Clefairy, Gloom,
  Magneton, Poliwhirl and the rest no longer evolve by level. The card says
  which stone it is waiting for, and its farewell does not wait for one.
- **Gloom** takes two: a Leaf Stone makes Vileplume, a Sun Stone Bellossom.
- **The Dawn Stone** makes a male Kirlia into Gallade and a female Snorunt into
  Froslass. Kirlia still becomes Gardevoir, and Snorunt Glalie, by level.
- **Scyther** becomes Kleavor with the **Black Augurite** ($3,000), or Scizor
  by trade -- whichever comes first. A Scyther already set to evolve by trade
  keeps that path, and the augurite is not used up.
- A stone that would do nothing (wrong creature, wrong gender, or a creature
  your sprite packs cannot draw) is kept.

**Eevee follows the stones too.** Water, Thunder, Fire, Leaf and Ice give
Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Leafeon and Glaceon. Its other three come from your
bond: once its bond is 40 and it is at least two days old, it becomes Sylveon
if it knows a Fairy move, Espeon by day or Umbreon by night. Eevee no longer
evolves by level.

Bellossom, Gallade, Froslass and Kleavor no longer hatch from eggs: evolve
them instead.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged. A creature
already past its old stone level simply waits for its stone.
