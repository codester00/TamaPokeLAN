## v3.70 -- choose who leads

**The team picker now sets your battle order.** Before, your team always went in
with your current Pokemon first and then your party in slot order, whatever you
tapped. So there was no way to send one in second, for example a Smeargle that
should wait until the opponent has used a move it can Sketch.

Now the order you tap your Pokemon in is the order they fight in, and each one
shows its number: 1 leads. Tap one again to take it out and the rest move up.
The picker remembers your last team and its order for the next battle, even if
you move them around in your party.

**The picker has a scrollbar.** With seven Pokemon to choose from it has two
pages; the scrollbar on the right edge shows which one you are on, like every
other screen with pages. Drag along the edge to turn the page.

Nothing to do: update, then pick your team in the order you want it.
