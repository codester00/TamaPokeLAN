## v3.77 -- one-off encounters

Some creatures in the games were never in the grass: they stood on the map and
were met once. They stand in Explore now.

- **Snorlax** blocks Routes 12-15 and Routes 16-18 (level 30).
- **Two Electrode** wait in the Power Plant (level 34), after Zapdos.
- **Sudowoodo** stands on Routes 36-37 (level 20).
- **Lapras** waits in Union Cave B2F on **Fridays only**, as in Crystal (level 20).
- **The red Gyarados** is in the Lake of Rage, and it is **always shiny** (level 30).
- **Kecleon** hides on Route 120 (level 30).

Each one is met for certain on the first Explore in its area once that area is
open. Missed it -- it fled, fainted or you ran? It is not gone: it comes back
now and then in that area until you catch it. All of them still hatch from eggs
too.

**The area markers now mean a meeting for certain.** An area reads
`! AREA !` while a one-off is waiting there, and `* AREA *` while a legendary's
first meeting is. Once you have met it, the marker goes, even if you did not
catch it -- it can still turn up, just not for certain. (Before, the star stayed
until the legendary was caught.)

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
