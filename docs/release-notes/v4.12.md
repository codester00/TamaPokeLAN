## TamaPoke v4.12

Fixes and changes from playtesting.

**Battles**

- **HP bars no longer dip and refill when a new Pokemon comes out.** After a faint, the incoming Pokemon's bar (or the opponent's next one) could drop toward empty and then refill a moment later, before anyone had attacked. It now just shows the right HP.
- **Dig, Fly, Bounce, Dive and Shadow Force work like the games.** The first turn says "dug a hole!", "flew up high!" and so on, and the Pokemon leaves the screen: up into the sky, down into the ground or the water, or it vanishes. While it is away, attacks aimed at it are avoided. Only the moves that can reach it still hit: Earthquake hits a digging Pokemon for double damage, Gust hits a flying one for double, Thunder, Hurricane and Sky Uppercut hit a flying one normally, and Surf hits a diving one for double. Nothing reaches Shadow Force. The next turn it comes back and strikes.
- **Running from a trainer now counts as a loss.** Running from a gym leader, the League, the Battle Zone, the Battle Hall or a LAN battle first asks if you are sure. If you leave, a gym or League battle ends with Mom's blackout screen (and ends a League run). In a LAN battle your rival is told you forfeited, and both win-loss records count it. Running from wild Pokemon works as before. Quitting because the other player stopped responding does not count as a loss.

**Trading**

- **You can trade straight from Bill's PC.** On the YOU GIVE screen, the middle of the ring now opens Bill's PC, the same one you use everywhere else, instead of paging through boxes.
- **Choosing a Pokemon shows its card first**, with its moves and stats, a TRADE button and a BACK button. Nothing is offered until you tap TRADE.

**Sleep**

- **Pokemon get up on their own in the morning.** Put to bed at night, a Pokemon sleeps in until 8:00. From 8:00 to 18:00 any sleeping Pokemon gets up once its energy is full. Before this, a Pokemon put to bed before 6:00 never woke up on its own. You can still wake it whenever you like, and putting it back to bed during the day is a nap.

**Settings**

- **You can set the day of the week.** SET TIME now shows the day: tap the right side for the next day, the left side for the one before. Some special encounters happen on certain days (Lapras on Fridays), and before this the board's day of the week was usually wrong.

**Screens**

- The bag no longer prints a page number over its last item. The scrollbar on the edge shows the page.
- The page dots at the bottom no longer cover the BACK button inside the Pokedex or a gym ladder.
- The Battle Zone header says BEST GAUNTLET RUN instead of BEST STREAK.

LAN battles need v4.12 on both boards. Trading works with v4.08 and later.
