## TamaPoke v4.09

**The Battle Zone, tuned after the first playtests.**

- **A BACK button on every Battle Zone screen and the Battle Hall.** Swiping down still works too.
- **Bigger text** for the lines under each title and inside the buttons, wherever it fits.
- **Cheaper entry**: $1,500 for the gauntlet and $2,500 for the tournament. One hard gym rematch pays for an entry.
- **Gauntlet bosses**: every 20th challenger is a boss, fully trained and at your level, with a team picked from the strongest Pokemon the rules allow. Beat the first for the silver symbol on your player card and the second for gold.
- **Gauntlet pay is steady**: 3 BP a win, every round, plus 10 for a boss. The day's daily run still pays double.
- **The tournament pays 10 BP more for winning the title.**
- **New prize prices**: PP Max and Bottle Cap now cost 120 BP (about one 40-win gauntlet run), and the Master Ball 800. PP Up, vitamins and the Linking Cord cost about what they do at the Mart in money.
- **PP Ups matter in the gauntlet.** Your team's PP carries from battle to battle, so PP Ups on your best moves are what get a strong team past the first boss.
- **The Battle Hall costs less energy** (6 a battle) and pays a little less money.

Nothing to do: your save, BP and records are untouched. Trading and LAN battles work with v4.08 boards.
