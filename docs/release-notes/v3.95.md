## v3.95 -- hard mode, retuned

**The hard Pokemon League can be beaten now, if you build for it.** Hard mode's
leaders used to fight fully trained, which meant only a flawless team ever
cleared the Elite Four and the champion. Each region's hard leaders now train
to a level picked for that region, so:

- a team chosen for its matchups, with strong individual stats, full training
  and its TMs, should win nearly every League run;
- a team of whatever creatures you happen to have, half trained, wins about one
  run in ten.

Hard leaders keep their perfect stats and their smarter battle choices. Easy
mode is unchanged.

**The hard gyms got easier too**, since they share the same leaders' training.

Nothing to do: your save and badges carry over unchanged.
