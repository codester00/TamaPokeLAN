## TamaPoke v4.00

**Swiping past the Mart works again.** A swipe across the screen that started near the edge, above or below the middle, was read as turning the Mart's page instead of moving on to the next screen. The Mart was the one place it bit, because its buttons push your finger out to the edge. Now a swipe that heads across the screen always moves you along, wherever it starts. Dragging along the edge still turns pages as before, on every screen that has them.

Nothing to do: install and swipe.
