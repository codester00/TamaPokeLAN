## v3.84 -- Stones in the wild

**Evolution stones can now be found after wild battles**, in the places where
the original games leave them lying around: the Moon Stone in Mt. Moon, the
Thunderstone in the Power Plant, the Fire Stone on Mt. Ember, the Water Stone in
the Seafoam Islands, Solaceon Ruins' Fire, Water and Thunder Stones, the Shiny
Stone on Iron Island, and more across Kanto, Johto, Hoenn and Sinnoh.

- About one win in ten in such an area finds one of its stones. Everywhere else
  a win never finds a stone, so they stay something to go looking for.
- The Mart still sells every stone. The Ice Stone and the Black Augurite, and
  every stone in Unova onward, come from the Mart only.
- The Safari Zone and the Great Marsh never hand out items, so their Leaf Stones
  stay in the Mart too.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
