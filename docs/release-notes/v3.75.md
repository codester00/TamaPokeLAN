## v3.75 -- the Safari Zone

Kanto's **Safari Zone** now plays the way it did in FireRed and LeafGreen.

- **No team.** Your creatures stay home: nothing of yours fights, so a Safari
  encounter costs no energy and no PP.
- **30 Safari Balls every encounter.** They are the Safari's own: your bag's
  Poke Balls are never used, and leftovers do not carry over.
- **BALL / BAIT / ROCK / RUN.** Bait makes a creature less likely to run but
  harder to catch while it eats; a rock makes it easier to catch but likelier
  to run while it is angry. Running always works.
- **The odds are the games' own**: every species has its real catch rate, and
  the ones FireRed made skittish (Chansey, Scyther, Pinsir, Tauros,
  Kangaskhan, Dratini, Dragonair and more) flee as often as they did there.
- No entry fee and no step limit: each Explore tap is one encounter, as in
  every other area.

Fuchsia City's pond now belongs to **Routes 12-15** instead of the Safari
Zone, so the Safari Zone holds only its own creatures. In Sinnoh, Pastoria
City's water joined **Route 213**; it was the same water already, so nothing
you can meet there changed.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
