Sinnoh is a real place to explore, and its legendaries have homes. Your save
carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Sinnoh's 54 areas**, taken from Platinum -- Route 201, Eterna Forest, the
  Old Chateau, Mt. Coronet, the Lost Tower, the Great Marsh, Iron Island, the
  three lakes, Turnback Cave, Victory Road, the Battle Zone and the rest --
  each opening with the badge that opened it in the game, with levels that
  follow Sinnoh's gym leaders. A locked area says which badge it needs, like
  REQUIRES FOREST BADGE.
- **Day and night matter in Sinnoh too.** As in Platinum, some creatures come
  out only in the morning or at night, like Kricketot on Route 201. If the
  clock was never set, it counts as day.
- **Sinnoh's legendaries live somewhere now.** Uxie, Azelf, Giratina and the
  roaming Mesprit appear after the Icicle Badge; Dialga, Palkia, Heatran,
  Regigigas and the roaming Cresselia after you become champion. The first
  visit to a legendary's home after its badge is a guaranteed meeting, and it
  keeps turning up now and then until you catch it. Regigigas is level 1, just
  as it was in Platinum.
- **Sinnoh's legendaries are caught, not hatched.** Eggs no longer hold them.
  Manaphy, Phione, Darkrai, Shaymin and Arceus, which have no home in the
  story, still come only from eggs.
- **Storyline progression locks adapted from the real games' data** reach
  Sinnoh too.

Unova and the regions after it keep the old whole-region encounters for now.
