**Gym, Elite Four, champion and LAN battles now happen indoors.** Gym leaders
fight you in the gym, each Elite Four member and the champion in their own
room, and LAN battles take place in the Battle Frontier -- the rooms from
Pokemon Emerald, in every region. They used to borrow the outdoor scene of the
opponent's first Pokemon. Wild battles still happen outdoors.
