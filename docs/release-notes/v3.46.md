Alola is a real place to explore, and its legendaries have homes. Your save
carries over; back up first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **Alola's 54 areas**, taken from Sun and Moon -- Route 1 and Hau'oli,
  Verdant Cavern, Ten Carat Hill, Brooklet Hill, Wela Volcano Park, the Lush
  Jungle, Mount Hokulani, the Haina Desert, the Thrifty Megamart, Vast Poni
  Canyon, Mount Lanakila, Resolution Cave and the rest. Alola has trials
  instead of gyms, so each area opens with the trial that opened it in the
  game, and a locked area names the Z-Crystal it needs, like
  REQUIRES WATERIUM Z. Levels follow the island challenge.
- **Calls for help count.** Creatures you could only meet when a wild one
  called an ally -- Pikachu, Mareanie, Goomy, Castform, Hakamo-o and others --
  turn up now and then on their own, as do the ones hiding in rustling grass,
  dust clouds and bird shadows.
- **Alola's legendaries live somewhere now.** Solgaleo and Lunala wait at the
  top of Vast Poni Canyon after Nanu's grand trial; the four island guardians
  beside their ruins after you become champion. The first visit to a
  legendary's home after it opens is a guaranteed meeting, and it keeps
  turning up now and then until you catch it.
- **The Ultra Beasts are found, not hatched.** Each of the seven waits in its
  own place after you become champion, the way Looker's quest finds them, and
  Necrozma waits in Ten Carat Hill. They no longer come from rare eggs.
- **Type: Null and Cosmog still come from eggs**, as do Magearna, Marshadow
  and the other mythicals.
- **Storyline progression locks adapted from the real games' data** reach
  Alola too.

Galar and Paldea keep the old whole-region encounters for now.
