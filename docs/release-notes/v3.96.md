## TamaPoke v3.96

**Bill's PC no longer moves a creature you only looked at.** Tapping a creature on the party screen used to open its sheet and also quietly mark it, so opening the PC next put that creature in the PC. Now tapping only shows the sheet, and the PC button only opens the PC. To store a creature, open its sheet and tap the new **TO PC** button. RELEASE is narrower, on the right, so it is harder to hit by mistake.

**BACK buttons are easier to hit.** Every BACK button in the Pokedex, the region chooser and GO TO # is taller, and a tap just above one no longer lands on the row above it.

**Training costs what it pays.** A session's energy and food cost now follow what it actually earned: a full session costs 12 energy, a small one less, and a round that earned nothing costs nothing. The two-stat games pay more, so they cost more. A creature under 15 energy is **TOO TIRED** to train; the joy games still open.

**Every result screen says what you got.** The ball game finally shows the Defense it trained, and every training game now lists the joy and bond it gave too. A zero always says why (already at MAX, or the day's bond already earned).

**Ditto can Transform.** Ditto now knows **TRANSFORM**. In battle it becomes its opponent for that fight: look, type, stats, stat changes and moves. When the fight ends, or Ditto is switched out, it is a Ditto again. A Ditto you already have learns it by itself. It works in LAN battles, which need v3.96 on both boards.

**Rock Smash forgives a frozen screen.** If the board's picture stalls for a moment, the beat waits for it instead of counting a miss. The board now also reports what caused a stall on its serial console, to help track down a board that freezes now and then.

**Hard mode is tuned leader by leader.** Each hard leader now has its own strength, so each battle on its own is about as hard as intended: a well-built team wins almost every time, an ordinary one about a third of the time at a gym and about one time in ten against the Elite Four and the champions. Some early gym leaders stay beatable even at full strength.
