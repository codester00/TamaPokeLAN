150 new moves, and attacks that change stats. Your save carries over; back up
first and leave "Erase device" unchecked, as always.

- **150 new moves**, 239 in all. First come the real early attacks many
  creatures were missing: Powder Snow, Gust, Vise Grip, Fairy Wind, Leafage
  and more. Before, a lot of creatures had nothing to attack with until well
  into their lives; now almost every one can fight from the start, as in the
  games. Then the moves the most creatures learn: Take Down, Fury Swipes,
  Giga Drain, Aerial Ace, Brave Bird, Flare Blitz and many others.
- **Attacks can change stats now.** Bubble Beam may slow the target, Mud
  Shot always does, Metal Claw may raise your Attack, Close Combat and Overheat
  lower your own stats in return for their power. Psychic, Crunch, Shadow Ball,
  Acid and the other moves that were missing their side effect have it back.
- **The battle text says when a stat changes**: "CHARIZARD's ATTACK rose!",
  for Growl and Swords Dance too, which used to change things silently.
- **The gym list remembers easy or hard.** It used to open on easy after every
  restart, which made badges won on hard look missing. They never were.
- The moves list on the stats card shows a page number when a creature knows
  more moves than fit on a few pages.
