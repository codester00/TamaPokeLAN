## v3.80 -- gender

Every creature now has a gender, shown beside its name on its card and on the
party sheet: a blue male or a red female symbol.

- It is fixed for good when the creature hatches or is caught, and it travels
  with it when you trade.
- The games' own rules hold: Tauros and Hitmonlee are always male, Chansey and
  Jynx always female, and Magnemite, Staryu and the legendaries have no gender.
  Everything else is an even chance.

Gender decides three evolutions, as in the games:

- **Burmy** becomes Wormadam if it is female and Mothim if it is male.
- Only a **female Combee** becomes Vespiquen, and only a **female Salandit**
  becomes Salazzle. A male one stays as it is, and its farewell comes when its
  time comes rather than waiting for an evolution.

Mothim no longer hatches from eggs: raise a male Burmy instead.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged, and the
creatures you already have keep the gender their ID gives them.
