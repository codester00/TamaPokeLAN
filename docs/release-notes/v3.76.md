## v3.76 -- Safari in Hoenn and Sinnoh

Hoenn's **Safari Zone** and **Safari Extension** and Sinnoh's **Great Marsh**
now play like their games, the way Kanto's Safari Zone has since v3.75: no
team of yours, 30 Safari Balls every encounter, and each game's own menu.

- **Hoenn (as in Emerald): BALL / POKEBLOCK / GO NEAR / RUN.** Going near makes
  a creature easier to catch but more likely to run off; a Pokeblock makes it
  more likely to stay. There are no Pokeblock flavours here, so every block works
  like a middling one in Emerald.
- **Sinnoh (as in Platinum): BALL / BAIT / MUD / RUN.** As in Platinum, bait makes
  a creature easier to catch but usually more likely to flee, and mud the
  opposite. Each species runs off as often as it did in Platinum.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
