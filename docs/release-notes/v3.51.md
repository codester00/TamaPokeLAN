TMs are items now. Your save carries over; back up first and leave "Erase
device" unchecked, as always.

- **TMs are something you own.** Before, every TM move a creature could learn
  appeared for free once it reached level 40. Now a TM move is learnable once
  you have that TM, at any level, and a TM is never used up: own it once and it
  teaches every creature that can learn it. Moves your creatures already know
  stay where they are.
- **The Poke Mart sells five TMs a day**, a different five each day. A TM you
  own never shows up there again.
- **Wild battles can drop a TM** now and then, always one you do not have yet.
- Gym leaders and wild creatures still use their TM moves from level 40, as
  before.

Trading and LAN battles need both boards on this version.
