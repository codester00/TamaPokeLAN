## v3.79 -- prize money, and no more double evolutions

**Beating a trainer now pays**, as it did in the games. The win screen shows the
prize under the badge.

- Hard mode pays the games' own amount: 4 x the level of the trainer's last
  creature x 25, or x 50 for a champion. Brock pays $1,400, Giovanni $5,000,
  Kanto's champion $13,000.
- Easy mode pays half.
- Your first win over each leader pays in full; every rematch after that pays
  half. Your badges already count as first wins, so a leader you have beaten
  before pays the rematch rate.
- Wild battles, losses and LAN battles pay nothing.

**Six creatures evolved twice in a row.** If its second evolution's level was
at or below its first, a creature that had just evolved was offered the next
one straight away -- a Teddiursa went to Ursaring and then Ursaluna in two
taps. The second evolution now comes ten levels after the first:

- Ursaluna 40, Mamoswine 43, Kingambit 62, Azumarill 35, Blissey 40 and
  Hydrapple 40.

A creature already past its new level evolves at its next level-up. Some wild
Azumarill, Mamoswine and their kin now turn up at those levels too.

Nothing to do: update as usual. Your save carries over unchanged.
