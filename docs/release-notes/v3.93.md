## v3.93 -- a hidden move offer no longer takes your taps

**Fixed: a tap could teach your creature a move you never saw offered.** When
your creature levelled up and wanted to learn a move while you had another
screen open -- the party, the move list, the Pokedex, the training games, the
keyboard or the clock -- that screen stayed on the panel, but your taps went to
the invisible "wants to learn" question underneath. Tapping a party slot or a
key could quietly replace one of your creature's moves. Now a tap always goes
to the screen you can see, and the move offer is waiting for you when you
close it.

**Fixed: the diagnostics named some screens "(null)".** The serial console's
health report and the crash report now name Bill's PC, the GO TO # pad and the
other screens added recently.

Nothing to do: your save carries over unchanged, and boards on v3.92 still
trade and battle with this one.
