## TamaPoke v4.07

**The Battle Hall opens on the PLAZA.** Take your pet into a one-on-one challenge against all 18 types, one at a time, in a different order every run. It's open to everyone, not only after the champions.

- **It starts gently**: the first opponents are a few levels below your pet and untrained, and each one after is a little stronger.
- **Each battle costs energy.** When your pet is tired, let it rest and come back: your run is saved between battles. A nap also refills its moves, since the Hall uses your pet's own PP.
- **Every win pays a little money.** A loss ends the run, but there's no blackout.
- **Clear all 18 and your pet is a HALL CHAMPION**, shown on its card for good. The mark stays with it when it joins your party, goes to the PC, or is traded.
- **The run belongs to one pet.** Changing your live pet ends it, and so does turning the board off in the middle of a battle.

**Trading and LAN battles with this version need the other board on v4.07 too.**

Nothing else to do: your save is untouched.
