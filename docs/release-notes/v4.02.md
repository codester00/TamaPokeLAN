## TamaPoke v4.02

**You choose who comes on after a faint.** When one of your creatures faints and you have more than one left, the battle asks who goes next. Before, the next one in line always came on, whether it suited the fight or not. Choosing doesn't cost you a turn. If you only have one left, it comes on by itself as before.

**Fights against trainers now ask before their next creature comes on.** When a trainer's creature faints, the battle tells you what they're sending next and asks if you want to switch first. The switch is free. Tap NO to keep the one you have out.

**Hard trainers think about who to send.** On hard, a trainer picks their next creature to suit the one you have out, instead of always going in order. Easy trainers still go in order.

**LAN battles:** each player picks their own next creature after a faint, and the other board waits while they do. LAN battles don't offer the free switch before the rival's next creature, the same as link battles in the games. **Both boards need this version** to battle each other.
