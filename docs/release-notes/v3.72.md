## v3.72 -- Kadabra learns its moves, and gets its farewell

**Pokemon that evolve only by trade now get their farewell.** A Kadabra,
Haunter, Graveler, Machoke and the like waited for an evolution that only a
trade can bring, so they were never offered a farewell, even days after level
100. Now they are offered one like any final form. A Pokemon you have traded
or given a Linking Cord still evolves first, then says goodbye.

**A Pokemon learns its moves when it evolves.** Evolution waits for you to
tap your Pokemon, and the moves its new form learns at earlier levels used to
count as already missed. An Abra that evolved late became a Kadabra that knew
nothing, all the way to level 100. Now an evolving Pokemon learns every move
its new form has passed: empty slots fill on their own, and the rest are
offered one at a time, as on a level-up. Moves you already turned down are not
offered again. A Pokemon you make your current Pokemon also fills any empty
move slots this way. You can still change moves any time on the MOVES page.

**The level bar reads MAX LEVEL at 100.** It used to count down the minutes
to level 101, which never comes.

Nothing to do: update. A Pokemon already past its farewell age shows the
farewell button the next time it is awake.
