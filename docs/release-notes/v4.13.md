## TamaPoke v4.13

**Your Pokemon keeps living while the board is switched off.** Switching the
board off to save battery used to put your Pokemon's life on hold in an odd
way: a Pokemon that was asleep when you switched off slept through the whole
gap, however long, and woke at about half on everything; one that was awake
never dropped below a safe floor and never made a care mistake. A Pokemon put
to bed at night and left with the board off until the next evening was still
asleep at 19:30.

Now the time the board was off is played exactly as if it had stayed on. It
sleeps at night, sleeps in until 08:00, gets up for the day, gets hungry and
tired, and if it is forgotten, care mistakes and running away happen at the
same times they would with the board on. **The 8-hour rule still protects
it**: get all four needs to 90 or more and a school or work day with the
board off is just as safe as with it on.

**If you forget bedtime, it passes out.** From 18:00 a Pokemon that is still
awake stays up until it is completely worn out. Once its energy has been as
low as it goes for 2 hours of night, it passes out on the floor, at 20:00 at
the earliest. That counts as a care mistake, and it sleeps badly: food, joy
and hygiene keep falling all night, and it is up at 06:00 with no sleeping in,
rested but hungry, dirty and sad. One that still has energy at dawn stays up
all day, grumpy and tired. Put it to bed and none of this happens.

**Turning the screen off no longer puts it to bed.** Between midnight and 06:00
the screen being off used to send it to sleep. Bedtime is the Light button now
(or passing out).

**Disturbed at dawn, it wakes up.** Between 06:00 and 08:00, while it sleeps
in, petting it or tapping food, bath or training wakes it. Before, only the
Light button did.

**Fitness rises as high with the board off as with it on** (it used to stop
at 85 while the board was off).

Nothing to do: install and play. Your save carries over. A Pokemon that was
asleep in an old-style screen-off sleep still wakes when the screen comes on.
LAN battles and trading work with v4.12 boards.
